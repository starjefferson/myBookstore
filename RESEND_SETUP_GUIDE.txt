MY BOOKS — RESEND EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS GUIDE
============================================

PURPOSE
-------
Resend is the email delivery service for MY BOOKS. It sends branded emails
from the bookstore to:

1. The operations team when a customer pays or submits a custom book request.
2. The customer when their order/request is received.
3. The customer when the vendor supplies dispatch details.
4. The customer when the vendor/courier confirms delivery.

Resend does not read emails from Rovingheights, Retala, or a courier. Those
messages arrive in the team's business inbox. A team member reads the update,
finds the corresponding order in /admin, updates its status, and the app uses
Resend to send the customer an update.


THE OPERATIONS FLOW
-------------------

PAID CATALOG ORDER

Customer checks out and pays with Paystack
    -> The app verifies the Paystack payment on the server
    -> The app creates one paid order in Firestore
    -> Resend emails the operations team: "New paid order"
    -> Resend emails the buyer: "We received your order"
    -> Team buys the exact item from Rovingheights or Retala using:
         - the buyer's name, phone number, and delivery address
         - the team's business email address for vendor updates
    -> Vendor sends dispatch/tracking details to the team's business inbox
    -> Team opens /admin, marks the order as ordered/dispatched, and enters
       any tracking details received from the vendor
    -> Resend emails the buyer with the vendor-provided dispatch details
    -> Vendor/courier emails the team that delivery is complete
    -> Team marks the order as Delivered in /admin
    -> Resend emails the buyer that delivery has been confirmed

CUSTOM BOOK REQUEST

Customer submits a book request from /order-request
    -> The request is saved in Firestore with status "order_requested"
    -> Resend emails the operations team: "New book request"
    -> Resend emails the customer: "We received your request"
    -> Team contacts the customer if the book can be sourced and confirms the
       price/payment process manually.


WHAT THE APP ALREADY DOES
-------------------------

The existing route app/api/send-shipping-email/route.js and helper
lib/resend.js are for dispatch notifications. When an admin enters a courier
name and tracking/waybill number in /admin, the app can send a branded email
to the buyer's saved buyerEmail address.

The following emails still need to be added to complete the notification flow:

1. Internal operations alert for each verified paid order.
2. Internal operations alert for each custom book request.
3. Buyer order-received confirmation after a verified paid order.
4. Buyer request-received confirmation after a custom book request.
5. Buyer delivered confirmation after an admin marks the order Delivered.


RESEND ACCOUNT SETUP
--------------------

1. Create an account at https://resend.com

2. In Resend, add and verify a sending domain that you own, for example:
   mybooks.ng

3. Resend will provide DNS records. Add them at the company where your domain
   is managed. These normally include SPF and DKIM records. Wait until Resend
   shows the domain as verified.

4. Choose a sender address on that verified domain, for example:
   orders@mybooks.ng
   hello@mybooks.ng

5. Create a Resend API key. Give it sending permission only where possible.

6. Add these variables to the hosting provider's Production environment
   settings (for example, Vercel Project -> Settings -> Environment Variables):

   RESEND_API_KEY=re_your_real_resend_api_key
   RESEND_FROM_EMAIL=orders@mybooks.ng
   ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL=you@yourteamemail.com

   Never place RESEND_API_KEY in a NEXT_PUBLIC_ variable. It must stay on the
   server and must never be pasted into browser code or committed to Git.

7. Add the same values to .env.local for local development. Do not commit
   .env.local to source control.


SENDER AND RECIPIENT RULES
--------------------------

FROM ADDRESS
The app sends from RESEND_FROM_EMAIL, e.g. orders@mybooks.ng. It must use a
domain verified in Resend. The default onboarding@resend.dev sender is suitable
only for early testing and has recipient restrictions.

TEAM ALERT RECIPIENT
The app sends internal alerts to ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL. This can be one
email address at launch. Later it can be a group mailbox such as
orders@mybooks.ng or multiple team addresses.

CUSTOMER RECIPIENT
The app sends buyer notifications to buyerEmail stored on the order. Checkout
requires the email address before payment. The custom request form also
requires it.


ADMIN DASHBOARD PROCESS
-----------------------

1. Sign in at https://your-domain.com/admin using the authorized Firebase
   admin account.

2. In "Paid (Needs Sourcing)", open the order and use its vendor link to buy
   the book manually from Rovingheights or Retala.

3. Enter the buyer's delivery address and phone number on the vendor site.
   Use the team's business email for vendor receipts, dispatch emails, and
   delivery confirmations.

4. When the vendor supplies tracking information, select the order in /admin,
   mark it as ordered/dispatched, enter the vendor/courier name and tracking
   number, then send the buyer update.

5. When the team receives a delivery-confirmation email from the vendor or
   courier, locate the order in /admin and mark it Delivered. The app should
   then send the buyer a delivery-confirmation email.

Do not invent tracking numbers. Only send the tracking information supplied by
the vendor or courier.


RECOMMENDED EMAIL SUBJECTS
--------------------------

To the team:
  New paid order #ORD-... — action required
  New custom book request — action required

To the buyer:
  We received your MY BOOKS order #ORD-...
  We received your book request
  Your MY BOOKS order has been dispatched
  Delivery confirmed for order #ORD-...

FREE PLAN AND LAUNCH GUIDANCE
-----------------------------

Resend's free transactional-email plan is suitable for an MVP. Check current
limits and pricing before launch at https://resend.com/pricing. Start with
email notifications; SMS and WhatsApp normally have per-message costs and
additional setup requirements.


SECURITY AND PRIVACY
--------------------

- Keep API keys server-side only.
- Send customers only the information they need: their order, status, and any
  vendor-provided dispatch details.
- Do not expose vendor invoices, source purchase receipts, supplier prices, or
  supplier order links to buyers.
- Ensure Firebase/Firestore access rules protect customer addresses and phone
  numbers.
- Use a real monitored team inbox for ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL so a person can
  act on every order alert.
